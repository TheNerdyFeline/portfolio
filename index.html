Where I am Now


January 11, 2025

It has been quite the journey since I taught myself web hacking starting in 2018. After 2 years of training, I got a job Bugcrowd working as as an Application Security Engineer in the Security Operations Triage department. I started in May 2020, and I have been increasing my skill level and adding to my skill set while working there. In 2023 I started learning hardware hacking. I have been taking online classes, and I was also mentored by another hardware hacker working in the same department. He was in charge of all hardware triage, and I was slowly taking on some of those programs. After he left Bugcrowd I took over. In June of 2024 I started handling all hardware triage, which has been a great learning opportunity.

In last 2 years I have participated in triaging 4 different hardware bashes around the US for multiple customers, with plans to attend more this coming year. We have tested several types of hardware at these bashes such as hotel kiosks, 5G networks, SCADA based operational technology, and various routers. Part of my job is to triage reports that come in for those events, and I ensure the hackers get what they need to look for any potential vulnerabilities. I also help the customers and hackers work together for a successful event to help build up their cybersecurity resilience. These events also include getting to work with some of the best hardware hackers from around the world. They have taught me so much about hacking, and like most hackers they are very welcoming. Those friendships have helped me as I took over all of the remote hardware programs, which include IOT devices, medical equipment, and various routers. That list continues to grow, which is one of the best parts!

It has been a long 6 years, and despite all the chaos it has been some of the best days of my life! Hardware hacking had been on my list of things to learn for years, and thanks to Bugcrowd it became a reality. While I won't stay at Bugcrowd forever, it is a great place to learn and grow as a hacker. I have learned so much, and I am looking for what new adventures await me both with Bugcrowd and in the wonderful world of cybersecurity. I am going to be a certified hardware hacker soon, and I can't wait to see what new adventures that brings. I have also started taking self defense based martial arts, and at the end of December I earned my orange belt in a mix of Karate and Jiu-Jitsu. Well off to learn, hack, jab and throw, TTFN.
